My Penny Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026 · Bundle ID com.mayooran.penny

My Penny (“the app”) is a personal finance planner for iPhone. The developer does not collect data from this app. That matches the App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Data stored only on your device

Information you enter — income, bills, budgets, savings goals, debts, transactions, display name, currency, and settings — is saved locally with Apple’s SwiftData / on-device storage. Optional bill reminders are scheduled locally with Apple’s notification system. This data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it. The developer does not receive it and cannot recover it.

What we do not do

  • No bank connections or credential collection
  • No developer-operated servers or user accounts
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of personal or financial data
  • No tracking across apps or websites for advertising
  • No App Tracking Transparency / advertising identifier use

Purchases (Penny Pro)

If you buy Penny Pro (subscription or lifetime unlock), the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment card details. Apple’s purchase records are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no chat, social feed, or account system.

Your choices

  • Use the app without enabling notifications.
  • Reset demo data or delete all data from Settings.
  • Delete the app to clear all local financial data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. Material changes that affect data practices will also be reflected in the App Store privacy labels.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the Support page, or email the developer address listed on the App Store product page for My Penny.
