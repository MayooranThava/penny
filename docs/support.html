My Penny Support

Private budgeting for iPhone · com.mayooran.penny

Quick help

  • First launch: Choose “Explore with sample data” to try a populated household, or start fresh with your own numbers.
  • Safe to Spend: Set monthly take-home income in Settings, add bills and spending, then check Home for what’s left.
  • Currency: Settings → Currency (CAD, USD, GBP, EUR, AUD).
  • Bill reminders: Settings → Notifications (optional; permission stays on-device).
  • Widgets: Long-press Home Screen → Add Widget → Penny (Safe to Spend / upcoming reminder).
  • Penny Pro: Settings → Unlock Penny Pro. Restore purchases from the same screen if you reinstall.
  • Reset / delete data: Settings → Data. Deleting the app also clears local data permanently.

Privacy

Penny stores your financial information on your device only. Read the full Privacy Policy.
